# Working in This Repo

This is Matt's (the GM's) lore repo for a **Shadowdark** campaign set in and below **Alanthaeris**, a floating city. Start with [README.md](README.md). Then read [gm/party.md](gm/party.md), [open-questions.md](open-questions.md), and [decisions.md](decisions.md).

## Conventions

- **Status tags:** ✅ decided, 🟡 leaning or proposed (needs Matt's confirmation), ❓ open. Mark anything you invent as 🟡. Never present it as decided.
- **When Matt decides something:** update the relevant page, add an entry to `decisions.md` (newest first, dated), and remove or adjust the item in `open-questions.md`.
- **Spoilers:**
  - `players/` holds player-facing summaries only. Never put secrets there.
  - `gm/` holds secrets, scenes, and session notes.
  - `faith/` and `world/` are mostly player-safe, but check before sharing.
- **`sources/`** is raw, non-canon inspiration (Mike's brainstorm, Discord notes). Some of it is outdated. Canon lives in the pages.
- **Commit** after each change, with a short message.

## Matt's Preferences

- **Theology:** avoid overtly Christian framing; undertones are fine. Prefer bird, wind, and weight metaphors. ("Confession" is fine. "Molting" was rejected.)
- **Tone of the faith:** beautiful and sincere, but crafted to build loyalty to the Twelve.
- **Names:** Alanthan humans use Greek-flavored names. Dwarves use their own naming style. Rejected: "Under Her wings," "Hypsele," "Feather" as a name for a part of the Codex, and the loom imagery.
- **Speeches and scenes:** keep them brief and readable at the table. No condescension toward adult PCs. Religious rather than complaining.
- **Mike's material** (the houses, House Evgenios) is adopted, but not set in stone.

## ⚠️ Open Concern

The repo has a GitHub remote, and Matt has shared links to player pages. If the repo is public, or players can browse it, then everything in `gm/` is exposed. Confirm with Matt before suggesting he share links.
