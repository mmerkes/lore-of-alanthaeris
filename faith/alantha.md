# Alantha

[← Home](../README.md)

✅ **Alantha** is the goddess worshipped in Alanthaeris. The faith's symbolism centers on **rising to the skies**: elevation, breath, open air, and being held aloft.

## What the Faithful Believe

- 🟡 Alantha is the one true source of divinity. The old gods were lesser powers, or they drew their power from her.
- 🟡 Alantha lifted the city into the sky, and she keeps it there. **Faith is what keeps you from falling.**
- 🟡 The road to the afterlife runs through Alantha.
- 🟡 The surface world awaits the faith, and it is sacred duty to bring the faith to it (see [The Pilgrimage](pilgrimage.md)).

## Titles

- 🟡 **The All-Mother**: her primary title
- ❓ Other epithets from the brainstorm, like the Wellspring and the Sky-Loom, need review. The loom imagery was de-emphasized.

## Symbols & Imagery

❓ Nothing is settled yet. The candidates from the brainstorm that fit the sky theme best are:

- **The Impossible Flame**: flames pointing *downward* toward the floating city
- **Glass-floor sanctums**: prayer chambers that look down into the abyss
- **Resonance-crystal domes**: open-air domes that chime in the wind

## Sayings (candidates)

- ❓ "May She hold us high." (said during turbulence or fear)
- ❓ "Heavier than surface-dust." (said of something worthless)
- ❓ "By the Firmament's grace."
