# Alantha

[← Home](../README.md)

✅ **Alantha** is the goddess worshipped in Alanthaeris. Her faith is about **flight**: rising, being held aloft, and being chosen to live above the world.

## Tone

✅ The faith is **genuinely beautiful**, with wind-song, white feathers, open sky and glass. Its imagery and teachings are **built to encourage loyalty to the Twelve**. Every image that makes a worshipper feel chosen also teaches them that the order of things (the Twelve, the rings, their own station) is holy and must not be disturbed.

## What the Faithful Believe

- ✅ The people of Alanthaeris are **chosen**. Alantha lifted them above the world, and that makes them **superior** to those below.
- ✅ The surface world is **unfortunate**. Surface people are pitied rather than hated. They are the **Flightless**: poor souls who were never lifted.
- 🟡 Alantha holds the city up on her wings, and the **Twelve are the feathers of those wings** (see below). Faith keeps the city aloft, and so does order.
- ✅ **Holiness is lightness; wrongdoing is weight.** 🟡 This isn't a sin-and-guilt system. Weight is a kind of *drag* that builds up from greed, fear, disorder, clinging to earthly things, and stepping out of your place. A heavy soul is hard for Alantha to carry. The fix isn't forgiveness but **shedding** the weight, through **confession**, discipline, duty and ritual.
- 🟡 When the faithful die, they **take wing** and join the High Air, the winds that circle forever above the city. Heavy souls sink toward the Dust.
- 🟡 Being chosen brings an obligation: to carry the faith to the Flightless (see [The Pilgrimage](pilgrimage.md)).

> **Design note:** Avoid overtly Christian theology. Undertones are fine. Prefer bird, body, wind and weight metaphors over guilt, sin, salvation and redemption.

## Holy Symbol: The White Swift

✅ The holy symbol is a **bird with its wings spread**.

🟡 That bird is **the White Swift**. Real swifts almost never land; they eat, drink and even sleep on the wing. The White Swift is the bird that **never touches the ground**, just like Alanthaeris. It's believed to be Alantha's messenger, or her earthly form. Seeing one is a blessing.

🟡 In the symbol, the swift's wings sweep up into a crescent. **Each wing has six primary feathers, twelve in total.**
- One wing stands for the six **arcane** houses, and the other for the six **divine** houses.
- The teaching behind it: *"Pluck one feather and the wing fails."* Every child who learns the symbol learns that the city stays up only as long as all twelve houses do.

## Titles for Alantha

- 🟡 **The All-Mother**: her primary title
- 🟡 **She Who Upholds**: used in liturgy
- 🟡 **The Wide-Winged**: used in poetry
- 🟡 **The Wellspring**: used by scholars, as the source of all divinity

## The Language of Wings

This vocabulary runs through everyday speech. 🟡 (Everything in this section is proposed.)

| Concept | How it's said |
|---|---|
| Alanthans | *the Winged*, or *the Held* |
| Surface people | *the Flightless*, or *the Grounded* (said with pity) |
| Children | *fledglings* |
| Coming of age | *Fledging* |
| Virtue | lightness, "going lightly" |
| Wrongdoing | *weight*, *drag*. A person carrying it is "heavy." |
| Penance, purification | ✅ *confession*: speaking your weight aloud so it can be shed |
| Dissenters | *the clipped* |
| Casting someone out | *grounding* |
| Knowing your station | "every feather in its place" |
| Death, for the faithful | *taking wing*: "Her grandmother took wing last winter." |
| The afterlife | *the High Air*, where the circling winds carry the dead |
| The pilgrimage | *the Descent* |

## Everyday Sayings

🟡 Proposed:

- **"Under Her wings."** A greeting. The reply is ***"And held high."***
- **"Fly true."** A farewell.
- **"Go lightly."** A blessing, or a gentle warning not to sin.
- **"May She hold us high."** A prayer said during turbulence or fear.
- **"By Her wings!"** An oath.
- **"Every feather in its place."** Means all is as it should be. It's also a quiet reminder to know your station.
- **"Pluck one feather and the wing fails."** Loyalty to the order, and to the Twelve.
- **"Heavier than surface-dust."** Said of something worthless.
- **"Sink me." / "Dust take it."** Curses.
- **"Flightless."** An insult for someone ignorant or crude.

## Look & Feel

🟡 Proposed:

- **Colors:** white, pale sky blue and silver. Brown and black suggest the ground.
- **Materials:** white feathers, glass, crystal and open air.
- **Sounds:** wind-chimes and resonance crystals, and hymns sung *upward*.
- **Sacred numbers:** 12 (the feathers and the houses) and 3 (the rings).
- **Sacred spaces:** temples open to the sky, and glass-floor sanctums that look down into the abyss.
- **Devotional tokens:** white feathers worn in the hair or on clothing.
- **Secondary motif:** *the Impossible Flame*, temple lamps that burn **downward** as a sign of her power over falling.
