# Church Hierarchy

[← Home](../README.md)

In Alanthaeris, church and state are the same thing. The houses that run the faith also run the city.

```
High Priest/Priestess  ── ceremonial head (hereditary)
        │  (controlled by)
   The Twelve          ── ruling houses (hereditary)
        │
   The Wardens         ── working clergy, in branches
        │
   The Faithful
```

## The High Priest / High Priestess

✅ Decided:
- The title is **hereditary** and passes through a single sacred family line.
- The role is **largely ceremonial**, like the emperors of Japan. The holder presides over rites and embodies the faith.
- **The Twelve largely control them.**
- ✅ They live in the **High Temple of Alantha**.

🟡 Nicknames include **the Apex** and others.

### The Current High Priestess

✅ **Aithra Ourania**
- *Aithra* comes from the Greek *aithēr*, the bright upper sky.
- *Ourania* means "heavenly." It's the name of the sacred line: **House Ourania**.

✅ The Ourania line is **separate from the Twelve**, but centuries of marriages have given it **plenty of Twelve blood**.

✅ Aithra is the niece of **Eudora Vassara**: one of Aithra's parents was a Vassara. She's close to Eudora and to Eudora's grandson, **Lexandros Evgenios**.

✅ In private, Aithra believes **the Twelve have drifted from Alantha's teachings**, and that the weight of their ways is dragging the city down.

## The Twelve (Keepers of the Book)

✅ Decided:
- The Twelve are the **twelve most powerful families** of Alanthaeris, and they rule both the church and the city.
- Each seat is **hereditary**. Every house descends from one of the **original twelve founders**, as far as anyone knows.
- All twelve houses are considered **priestly**.
- The houses keep a **half-arcane, half-divine** split: six arcane houses and six divine houses.
- **Every house guards its own secrets** for keeping the city flying and working. That makes each house hard to replace.
- The formal name is **Keepers of the Book**. People usually just say **the Twelve**.

🟡 Leaning: the **Book** is split into twelve parts, and each house holds one. The complete Book exists only when all twelve cooperate.

✅ House names, Keepers, and spheres are on [The Twelve Houses](twelve-houses.md).

❓ Open:
- How the Twelve reach decisions
- Whether a house has ever fallen or been replaced

## The Wardens

✅ The Wardens are the **lower order of clergy**. They do the helpful (and annoying) jobs around the city, and they're organized into branches named "Wardens of the ___."

✅ Some branches mix arcane and divine members, and some are made up of only one tradition.

| Branch | Role | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Wardens of the Nexus** | ❓ Keepers of the Door; they prepare pilgrims | 🟡 name, ❓ role |
| **Wardens of the Measure** | ❓ Inquisitors; they enforce orthodoxy and order | 🟡 name, ❓ role |
| ❓ *(third branch?)* | ❓ Rites, blessings, water/air purification | ❓ |

❓ Open: whether there are ranks within the Wardens, who can join (merit? any ring?), and whether enforcers are clergy or armed laypeople.

## Pilgrims

Pilgrims are **not a church rank**. They're individuals chosen for the descent. See [The Pilgrimage](pilgrimage.md).
