# The Pilgrimage

[← Home](../README.md)

This is the sacred descent from Alanthaeris to the surface world.

## What Pilgrims Are Told

- ✅ Pilgrims are chosen to descend to the surface through **the Door**.
- ✅ Their mission is to **find a prophesied someone or something** that carries deep symbolic meaning for the faith. They are **not to return until they find it.**
- ✅ Spreading the faith of Alantha is something pilgrims do **along the way**. It isn't the main goal.
- 🟡 The prophecy may be about a **"second coming."**
