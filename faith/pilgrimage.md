# The Pilgrimage

[← Home](../README.md)

The pilgrimage, also called **the Descent**, is the sacred journey from Alanthaeris down to the surface world.

## The Mission: The Lost Book

- ✅ Pilgrims are chosen to descend to the surface through **the Door**.
- ✅ Their mission is to **find the lost book of Alantha**, and they are **not to return until they find it.**
- ✅ The general populace knows only that the book holds **lost knowledge of Alantha**.
- ✅ Spreading the faith to the Flightless is something pilgrims do **along the way**. It isn't the main goal.

### Name

🟡 The recommended name is **the Fallen Feather**. The story goes that a feather fell from Alantha's wing when the city rose, and it was lost to the Dust below. That makes the book literally a piece of Alantha that the city left behind. The name ties into the swift symbol, and it explains why the book is on the surface.

Alternatives:
- **The Thirteenth Feather.** The Twelve could use this name privately. It hints that the book would add a thirteenth part to their twelve, which could be a threat as well as a treasure.
- **The Quill of Alantha.** This is a book written with a feather from her own wing.
- **The Book of the High Air.**
