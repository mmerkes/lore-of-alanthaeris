## The Houses

Each house holds one seat among the Twelve. The current holder of that seat is its **Keeper**. Each house's sphere is a **traditional responsibility**, not a hard limit on its authority.

## Customs

### Succession
🟡 Seats are hereditary *within* a house, but a Keeper's successor isn't automatic. Heirs build their claims, often through marriage. For example, Leontes Evgenios's marriage into House Drakaina strengthens his claim to succeed his father.

❓ Who actually chooses the successor: the sitting Keeper, the house, or the Twelve?

### Marriage & Names
✅ Marriages between the houses are political tools. They bind arcane to divine, house to house, or a house to useful families outside the Twelve.

✅ Surname rules:
- Children normally take their **father's** surname.
- When only **one parent is from a Keeper house**, the children take the Keeper surname.
- A **marriage contract** can fix the household's dynastic surname. Niketas and Lysandros did this, so their household uses Evgenios.

## House Evgenios in Detail

This is the family of **Lexandros Evgenios**, Mike's character.

### Family Tree

```
Theophanes Evgenios (Keeper) ═ Eudora Vassara (kin of Keeper Calliane Vassara)
│
├─ Kallistrate ═ Menekles Sarantios (kin of Keeper Iokastos)      → children are Sarantios
├─ Leontes     ═ Philomela Drakaina (kin of Keeper Melanippe)     → presumed heir
├─ Ianthe      ═ Adrasteia Kyrenios (no seat; architects)         → children are Evgenios
├─ Niketas     ═ Lysandros Asterion (kin of Keeper Praxion)       → Evgenios by contract
└─ Damon (5th child) ═ Thaleia Melanthios (no seat; artisans)
     └─ Lexandros (5th child), ordained priest, pilgrim
```

*(The birth order of Theophanes's children isn't set, except that Damon is the fifth.)*

### Marriages and What They Mean

| Marriage | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Theophanes & Eudora (Evgenios–Vassara) | An old alliance between arcane civic authority and divine orthodoxy. It's the foundation of the family's current influence. |
| Kallistrate & Menekles (Evgenios–Sarantios) | Ties the family to sacred law, the temple courts, and the enforcement of oaths |
| Leontes & Philomela (Evgenios–Drakaina) | A powerful arcane alliance that strengthens Leontes's succession claim |
| Niketas & Lysandros (Evgenios–Asterion) | Unites temple diplomacy with scholarship, medicine, and alchemy. Gives Niketas influential arcane allies. |
| Ianthe & Adrasteia (Evgenios–Kyrenios) | A love match that connects the family to the architects who maintain the city |
| Damon & Thaleia (Evgenios–Melanthios) | A modest match with prosperous craftspeople, not rulers |

These marriages balance three priorities: **political continuity**, **cooperation between the arcane and divine lineages**, and **practical ties to the people who physically keep the city running**.

Within the family:
- **Leontes** stands for succession.
- **Kallistrate** stands for divine law.
- **Niketas** stands for arcane diplomacy.
- **Damon's** branch stands for neither office nor alliance.

### Lexandros

- He's the fifth child of Damon, and Damon is the fifth child of the Keeper. That makes him part of a **junior branch**: respectable, useful, cherished, but not expected to matter.
- He's drawn entirely to the **priesthood**, which is unusual for an arcane house. It isn't rebellion.
  - His grandmother **Eudora Vassara** encouraged his spiritual education, and she sees his calling as her Vassara heritage blossoming.
  - His parents are proud, and his siblings love him.
  - Some Evgenios relatives quietly see his calling as rejecting the family's magical inheritance. It's a minor faux pas, not a scandal.
- He's connected to **five of the Twelve**: Evgenios, Drakaina, and Asterion (arcane), and Vassara and Sarantios (divine). The other seven houses know him through reputation, religious service, and his grandfather's position.
- **His selection as a pilgrim** had support from both sides. The divine Keepers see a sincere missionary. The arcane Keepers see an Evgenios entering the Door his own family maintains. Whether that support was an honor, a political compromise, or cover for a hidden purpose is uncertain.
- The Descent offers him a kind of significance he **can't inherit, marry into, or be granted** by his place in the family tree.
