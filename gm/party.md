# 🔒 The Party

[← Home](../README.md)

Everyone descends through the Door to **Iskiaeris** at the end of Session 1.

| Player | Character | Who they are | Official reason for descending | Secret / hook |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Mike** | **Lexandros Evgenios** | Ordained priest from a junior branch of House Evgenios (arcane); grandson of Keeper Theophanes and of Eudora Vassara | Chosen pilgrim: find the Lost Book of Alantha and bring lightness to the Flightless | High Priestess Aithra personally asked him to go, for Alantha rather than the Twelve. He doesn't know Terek is his bodyguard. His cousin Myrto was killed by Ryland. |
| **Josh** | **Meldren** | Dwarf, 135 years old, second-in-command of the Great Works; White Swift tattoo on his scalp | Ambassador of Alanthaeris to Iskiaeris | Investigate what's wrong at the bottom of the Great Chain before the public finds out |
| **Daniel** | **Terek** | Human fighter in his 60s; old Outer Ring mercenary; caretaker at heart, with one potted plant | (Vague to the table) | Eudora secretly hired him to protect Lexandros, and paid by healing his dying mentor, **Markin**. He left before knowing whether it worked. |
| **Elizabeth** | **Ryland St Vere** | Sword instructor to the great families | Fleeing | Killed **Myrto**, Lexandros's cousin, and Myrto's father, then ran through the Door during the Descent ceremony |
| **Bill** | **Reeve (Reevanous) Kallergis** | Aimless Inner Ring scion of House Kallergis, a lesser house | Penance, and restoring the family's name | Finish what his uncle started: recover House Kallergis's lost knowledge or the Lost Book. He knows the city is sinking. |

## Built-in Tensions

- **Ryland killed Lexandros's cousin**, and they arrive on the surface together.
- **Terek** is shadowing Lexandros for reasons the table doesn't know.
- **Lexandros and Reeve** both seek the Lost Book, one out of faith and one for the family's reputation, on a bet that it's a manual.
- **Meldren and Reeve** are both looking at the city's decline from different ends: the machinery and the magic.
