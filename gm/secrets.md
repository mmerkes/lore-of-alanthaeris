## The True Purpose of the Pilgrimage

✅ The pilgrimage has two purposes that the Twelve don't speak of:

1. **Finding and killing the prophesied figure.** The Twelve fear that this person will gain momentum and threaten their order, possibly by reuniting the city with the surface. They want the figure found and eliminated early. **Only the Twelve know this.**
2. **Getting rid of undesirables.** The pilgrimage is a convenient way to remove troublemakers.

⚠️ The pilgrims themselves do **not** know about either purpose.

## The Book

✅ The Book the Twelve keep is essentially a **giant maintenance manual for the city**. Each house guards its own part of how the city stays aloft and running.

## The Doctrine Was Designed

✅ The faith's beauty is sincere, but it was **crafted to build loyalty to the Twelve**. Its imagery and sayings are shaped so that devotion to Alantha and obedience to the ruling houses feel like the same thing. The clearest examples are the twelve feathers ("pluck one feather and the wing fails") and "every feather in its place."

❓ Who shaped it, and when? Was it the founders, or later generations of the Twelve? Do the Twelve still believe it themselves?

## Candidate Secrets (not decided)

These come from Mike's brainstorm and are **not canon**:

- ❓ The founding ritual **drained power from the old pantheon** to empower (or create) Alantha. The Twelve must periodically repeat it (the "Rite of Reconstitution").
- ❓ The surface has become a brutal hellscape, which is nothing like what the faith teaches.
- ❓ **No one has ever come back** through the Door.
- ❓ The founding prophet (the "messiah") may have been a demon or devil.
- ❓ A cloud barrier around the city carries anti-magic, which prevents counter-rituals from the surface.
- ❓ Secret shrines to the old gods exist in the Underspire.
