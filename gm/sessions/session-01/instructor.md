## Character

- A **sword instructor** who teaches the children of the great families
- 🟡 Either **paid by the family** and from the Outer or Middle Ring, or from a family with **no knack for magic** who learned the sword instead. That second option would give him a sore spot that this scene presses on.
- **By the end of Session 1:** he has killed a father and son, and he is running for the Door.

## The Victims

✅ **Myrto**, a teenage boy from a magic family who is bad at magic. He's too pretty, and he dies tragically.

✅ **Myrto is Lexandros's cousin** (Mike's character). That makes it House Evgenios, the arcane house.

🟡 **Myrto's father.** Pick one:

| Option | Who | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | **Leontes Evgenios**, Theophanes's presumed heir | Huge. The instructor kills the **heir to an arcane seat of the Twelve** and the heir's son. House Evgenios's succession is thrown open, House Drakaina loses its alliance, and the whole city hunts the killer. |
| **B** | **Niketas Evgenios** (married to Lysandros Asterion) | Contained, but still serious. The house of medicine and the Evgenios family both grieve. If Lysandros is Terek's healer, he's mourning a husband and son while reminding Terek of his duty. |

I'd go with **A** if you want Session 1 to end with the city in uproar. **B** keeps the fallout personal.

## Vignette 1: The Lesson

**Purpose:** show who he is and what he does.

**Setting:** A practice yard in the Inner Ring, maybe a sky-terrace with a glass railing and open air on every side. Wooden practice blades and wind-chimes.

### Beats

1. **The father arrives with Myrto.** The father is cold and disappointed. Myrto **failed in front of everyone at the ceremony**.
   - 🟡 The ceremony could be the **Fledging**, the coming-of-age rite where children of the arcane houses show their gift. Nothing came. Whispers about it have been going around the city.
2. **The punishment.** *"If he can't hold magic, he can at least hold a sword."* Sword training is a humiliation for an Evgenios, and the father wants it to hurt.
3. **The lesson.** Myrto is sullen, pretty, and bad at this too. The instructor teaches him. How he treats the boy (stern, kind, mocking) is the player's call.
4. **The father lingers.** He makes a remark that lands on the instructor, too: about people without the gift, or about hired men knowing their place. *"Every feather in its place."*

### Prompts for the player

- How does he treat Myrto when the father isn't looking?
- What does the father say that he can't let go?

## Vignette 2: The Run for the Door

**Purpose:** he goes crazy. Run it fast and chaotic.

### Beats

1. **Cold open, after the fact.** Blood on the practice yard, two bodies, and the player's hands. Let the player narrate as much or as little of the killing as they want.
2. **The alarm.** Bells. 🟡 The **Wardens of the Measure** are on the way.
3. **The chase.** He runs inward and downward through the city toward the Door. There are sky-bridges, crowded markets, glass-floored halls, and a Warden blocking the way. Run one or two quick obstacles as rolls or choices.
4. **The Door.** He bursts in during the **Descent ceremony**, with the pilgrims mid-anointing and the Door open. He goes through.

### Why did he snap? (❓ the player's choice, or yours to keep secret)

- **Rage:** years of contempt, and the father's last remark was one too many.
- **An accident that spiralled:** the lesson turned ugly, one of them was dead, and then there was no going back.
- **Something else:** a whisper, a compulsion. 🟡 This could be a thread for the old gods or something from Iskiaeris reaching up. It's a good mystery if the player is willing.
