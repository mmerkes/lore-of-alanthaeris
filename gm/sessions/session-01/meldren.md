# 🔒 Session 1 Vignettes: Meldren (Josh)

[← Home](../../../README.md)

## Character

- **Meldren**, a dwarf, age 135, about 4 feet tall
- His skin is darkened from a lifetime in the fire and light of **the Great Works**.
- His bald scalp carries an intricate tattoo of Alantha's holy symbol, **the White Swift**. His white beard reaches mid-chest.
- He is **second-in-command** under the Master of the Great Works.
- He is **faithful to Alantha**. Josh doesn't know the faith's details yet, so consider giving him the [player primer](../../../players/alantha-primer.md).
- **Official reason for descending:** he goes as Alanthaeris's ambassador to **Iskiaeris** to see what's happening there.
- **Real reason:** something is wrong in the Great Works, specifically with **the Great Chain**, and it must be fixed before the public finds out.

---

## Vignette 1: The Master of the Great Works

**Setting:** The Master's office deep in the Great Works. Forge-light, the constant clank of machinery, and the deep groan of the Great Chain somewhere below. The door is shut.

**Who's there:** Meldren and the **Master of the Great Works**.
🟡 Name: **Master Orrin Ashmantle**. He's old, blunt, and tired. He trusts Meldren more than anyone.

### What the Master tells him

1. **The Great Chain is failing its purpose.** It's the massive chain that hauls shipments up from the surface.
   - Officially, the Chain doesn't exist.
   - Everyone in the Great Works knows it does. Some things simply can't be had in the sky, like certain delicacies, ores and goods.
2. **Shipments are wrong.**
   - Expected loads haven't come up at all.
   - Things that make no sense *have* come up instead (see the list below).
3. **Nobody up here knows why.** Whatever is happening, it's happening at the **bottom** of the Chain.
4. **The cover story:** Meldren goes down as an official ambassador to Iskiaeris. That's true, but it isn't the whole truth.
5. **The real job:** find out what's going on at the bottom of the Chain and fix it, or at least get word back. Do it quietly. If this gets out, there will be panic, or worse, the Twelve will take notice.

### Strange things that came up the Chain

Pick one or two to show him. 🟡 These are all proposals.

- **A crate of river stones, each one carved with the White Swift.** One wing on every stone has only five feathers.
- **A cask that should have held wine is full of seawater** with a single black feather floating in it.
- **A bundle of old surface coins** from a mint no one recognizes, wrapped in a scrap of very old vellum covered in faded script. *(Optional hook: the script resembles the oldest verses of the Alanthiad. That's a faint thread toward the Lost Book.)*
- **An empty crate with a note scratched inside the lid:** *"Stop sending them."* No one knows who "them" refers to.

### Things the Master might hand over

- A writ naming Meldren as **Ambassador of Alanthaeris**
- A **Chain-token** or seal that whoever works the bottom of the Chain would recognize
- 🟡 The name of the last known contact at the bottom of the Chain

### Moments to play

- The Master pauses at the end: *"I'd go myself, but I'm too old, and you're too good to keep."* It's a compliment that's also a goodbye.
- Prompt for Josh: **What does Meldren ask the Master before he leaves?**

## Vignette 2: The Going-Away Party

**Setting:** A dwarven hall in the Great Works, or a tavern carved into the Underspire rock. Long tables, forge-warm air, ale, roasted meat, and loud songs.

**Tone:** On the surface it's a celebration of an honor and an official assignment. Underneath, everyone suspects it may be a one-way trip, and nobody says it out loud.

### Who's there

- Great Works crew: chain-hands, smiths, engineers
- 🟡 Meldren's family. Ask Josh who matters to him.
- 🟡 Possibly the Master, briefly. He doesn't stay long.

### Beats

1. **Toasts.** *"To Meldren, who goes where the Chain goes!"* *"May the sky keep you."* The reply is *"And you."*
2. **A downward lamp.** 🟡 Someone lights an *Impossible Flame* lamp, a temple lamp that burns downward, and sets it before him. It's an old Underspire custom for someone who descends: the light goes down with you. The lamp is meant to be kept burning until the person returns.
3. **The quiet tell.** An old chain-hand presses something into his hand, like a tool, a charm or a letter, and says *"for after."* They don't say after what.
4. **The unspoken thing.** Someone starts to ask *"When you come back—"* and is elbowed into silence.
5. **His moment.** Give Josh the floor: **Meldren's toast, or his farewell to the room.**

### Prompts for Josh

- Who at the party is hardest to say goodbye to?
- What does Meldren leave behind on purpose?

## GM Notes

- **The Great Chain is new lore.** It contradicts the idea that the Door is the *only* connection to the surface. Goods come up the Chain, and people go down through the Door. Is the Chain one-way, carrying cargo only? Has anyone ever ridden it?
- **Iskiaeris** is Josh's name for the place below. Is it the surface realm as a whole, a specific city, or the land the island was torn from?
- **A link to Aithra's scene:** Aithra says the city is "drifting" and heavier. The Great Works problem could be the physical side of that. If so, House Kallergis (flight) and House Evgenios (infrastructure) would care a great deal.
- **A link to the Lost Book:** if you use the vellum scrap, Meldren and Lexandros have a reason to compare notes once they're on the surface.
- **How does Meldren descend?** Through the Door with the pilgrims, or by the Chain?
