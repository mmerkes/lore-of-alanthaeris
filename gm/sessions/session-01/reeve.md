# 🔒 Session 1 Vignettes: Reeve Kallergis (Bill)

[← Home](../../../README.md)

## Character

- **Reevanous Kallergis**, who goes by **Reeve**
- He's from the **Inner Ring**, of **House Kallergis**: levitation, weather-working, and maintenance of the city's flight.
- He's aimless and not serious about much. He takes his privileged Inner Ring life for granted.
- He has **embarrassed the family** more than once. The Descent is his **penance**.
- He's not keen to go below, but it can't be helped. He can still be a jerk.
- His family is worried about sending him, along the lines of *"Why can't we send a servant?"*, but they're sending him anyway.

## What Reeve Knows

✅ The city was raised by **arcane and divine magic working together**.

✅ **The city is slowly sinking.** This isn't widely known. Inside House Kallergis, everyone knows, and **some blame the Kallergis family**.

✅ The **Twelvefold Codex** is, in part, a **maintenance manual**. Each house keeps its own part.

✅ The **dwarves** of the Great Works maintain the city's **non-magical machinery**. Kallergis works with them closely.

✅ **House Kallergis is a lesser house.** The original Kallergis line **died off or was taken over under suspicious circumstances**. When Reeve's branch took the seat, **some of the house's inherited knowledge was lost**. Their part of the Codex, the **Kallergeia**, has gaps, and the city is sinking.

✅ **An uncle of Reeve's went through the Door years ago** and never came back.

## Why the Lost Book Matters to Reeve

- If he finds it, it would **restore the family's reputation**.
- In game terms, it could **fill in the gaps** in House Kallergis's knowledge.
- He might find **other lost knowledge on the surface** that helps.
- If the book actually exists and he finds it, he'd be a **legend**.

## Vignette 1: The Head of the House

**Setting:** A Kallergis tower in the Inner Ring, slightly faded: fine furnishings that haven't been replaced in a generation. A great window looks out over the city, and a little lower than it used to, if you know where to look.

**Who's there:** Reeve and the **head of House Kallergis**.
🟡 **Theramenes Kallergis**, the Keeper. ❓ What is he to Reeve: father, grandfather, or uncle?

### Beats

1. **The summons.** Reeve is called in, probably expecting another lecture.
2. **"You're going through the Door."** Let Bill react naturally.
3. **Why his uncle went.** 🟡 The uncle went down years ago to recover the knowledge the house lost: the missing pieces of the Kallergeia, or the Lost Book itself. He never returned.
4. **The charge.** Finish what his uncle started. Find the Lost Book, or whatever else can fill the gaps. *"Find what we lost, and the family's name goes with you."*
5. **What's unsaid.** This is penance as much as a mission. Theramenes doesn't quite say *"and it gets you out of the way,"* but Reeve can hear it.
6. 🟡 **Optional sting:** Theramenes mentions the city's sinking almost in passing. *"You know what they say about us, out there."*

### Prompts for Bill

- How does Reeve take the news?
- Does he have any attachment to the uncle, or is the uncle just a family story?

## Vignette 2: Reeve's Last Day in the City

**The question:** *What does Reeve do on his last day in Alanthaeris?*

This one is Bill's to drive. Some prompts if he wants them:

- **A last indulgence:** a party, a lover, a gambling debt left unpaid
- **The uncle's old rooms,** untouched for years, possibly with a journal, a map, or a note
- **The Great Works:** a visit to the levitation engines with the dwarves. Maybe he runs into **Meldren**.
- **A glass-floor sanctum,** looking down at Iskiaeris for the first time and actually thinking about it
- **Saying nothing to anyone,** and simply walking the city

## GM Notes

- 🟡 **The uncle needs a name.** Suggested: **Demetrios Kallergis**. What happened to him below could be a campaign thread.
- **"Suspicious circumstances."** How did Reeve's branch take the seat? Was it inheritance by accident, or something darker? The brainstorm's *Schism of the Seventh Spellweaver* could be the story of the old Kallergis line.
- **Links to the other PCs:**
  - **Meldren** is investigating Great Chain problems that may be part of the same decline.
  - **Lexandros** is seeking the same Lost Book for faith rather than prestige.
  - **Aithra** said the city is "drifting." Reeve knows it's literally sinking.
- **Why the faith matters here:** the faithful believe the Lost Book brings people closer to Alantha, and that if their faith rises, the city will be saved. Cynics think it's an owner's manual. Reeve's family is betting on the cynics, while Lexandros carries the faithful view. That's a good built-in party tension.
