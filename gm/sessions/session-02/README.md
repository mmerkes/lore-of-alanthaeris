## Where We Left Off

The party came through the Door into **ruins in a forest** on Iskiaeris. The session ended on the *thwap* of bowstrings.

## Outline

1. **Ambush.** Goblins attack in the ruins, more than the party can handle.
2. **Rescue.** Villagers arrive with meager weapons and fight the goblins alongside the party.
3. **Welcome.** The villagers greet the party warmly as angels, and take them home to the village.
4. **Something is off.** Over several days, the cult's strangeness surfaces bit by bit.
5. **First night:** the hag's clay dolls. See [The Hag](#the-hag).

## Scene 1: Goblin Ambush

✅ Open with combat against **more goblins than the party can handle**.

✅ **Surface goblins are nasty and want to kill them.** This is the first shock of Iskiaeris. In Alanthaeris, goblins are kind, sweet, and soft-spoken, like house elves. The PCs have never seen a goblin be cruel.

🟡 Ideas:
- The goblins use the ruins as a hunting ground, because things come through the Door.
- Pick out one goblin that is *captured alive* by the villagers. It gets burned later (see [Smoke](#smoke-is-how-they-speak-to-alantha)).

## Scene 2: The Villagers Arrive

✅ Mid-fight, **villagers with meager weapons** (pitchforks, hatchets, slings) charge in and turn the battle.

✅ They are **cultists of a strange sect of Alantha**. They sincerely believe they're good people; their leader has manipulated them.

✅ They believe **anyone from the flying city is an angel sent down by Alantha**.

## The Village

✅ A **small, walled village in rough country**.
✅ A trail leads to a **bigger town, several days' journey** away.
✅ **No pub.** Instead, **each villager volunteers to take one PC home** to stay at their house. This splits the party at night, which matters for the hag.

❓ Village name. (Alanthan names are Greek; surface names can be something else.)
❓ Name of the bigger town.

### Host Families 🟡

One host per PC, each a small window into the cult. Ideas to assign:

| Host | Flavor | What's off |
|---|---|---|
| A widowed mother | Warm, feeds the PC far too much | Her two children went "on pilgrimage" last year. She reads their smoke every night. |
| A young couple | Giddy, newly married | They're praying to be chosen as the next pilgrim pair. |
| An old woodcutter | Gruff, practical | Quietly fasts to stay "light." He's gaunt and proud of it. |

## The Leader

✅ An **evil priest** with a **sincere, dopey facade** that makes people root for him. He has **flashes of being strict, serious, and dangerous**.

✅ **His secret:** he feeds villagers to **a demon in the woods** near the village. Periodically he chooses a **pair**, such as a romantic couple, siblings, or best friends, to go on **pilgrimage** to spread the word of Alantha. They're never heard from again. Everyone assumes they're out seeing the world. They're dead.

🟡 Ideas:
- The pairs mirror Alanthaeris's own pilgrimage. Lexandros, a real pilgrim, will notice.
- A send-off for the next pair is coming up, and he wants the angels to bless it. (See [Why Stay](#why-the-party-stays-a-few-days).)
- His tell: when someone questions doctrine, the dopey smile stays, but his voice drops and goes flat.

❓ His name.
❓ The demon: what it is, what it gives him in return, and where its lair is.
❓ Does he believe any of it, or is the faith purely a tool?

## What Makes the Cult Uncomfortable

✅ **They believe Alanthans are angels.**
✅ 🟡 **They commune with Alantha through smoke.** They awkwardly burn things.

### Smoke Is How They Speak to Alantha

🟡 The surface sect took Alanthaeris's own doctrine literally: **lightness is holiness, and smoke is the lightest thing there is.** Burning something makes it light and sends it up to her. This makes it a twisted mirror of the faith, not a random weirdness, and Lexandros can recognize the logic.

- Burning a written confession, a lock of hair, or a treasured object is how they shed weight.
- They read shapes in the smoke as Alantha's answers.
- 🟡 The captured goblin is **burned at the stake** to "make it light." The villagers think it's a mercy.
- 🟡 They smear ash on their foreheads, and on the angels' foreheads too.

### Other Uncomfortable Differences 🟡

Pick a few. Reveal them slowly, one or two per day.

1. **Relics of the angels.** Villagers quietly collect the PCs' shed hair, nail clippings, and used bandages, then keep them or burn them as offerings.
2. **Breath is holy.** Since breath is wind, they ask the angels to breathe on babies, charms, and seed grain.
3. **Weight is literal.** They fast to stay light. Heavy people are quietly suspect. Children are praised for being thin.
4. **Garbled scripture.** They recite fragments of the Alanthiad, but with words wrong and lines out of order. Where did they get it? 🟡 Perhaps an earlier pilgrim came through the Door long ago. (Reeve's uncle?)
5. **They give the angels everything.** The best food, the only bed, the children's shoes. The village can't afford it, and that makes the generosity uncomfortable.
6. **Angels go home by smoke.** The darkest escalation: the leader hints that when an angel's work on the ground is done, it must be returned to Alantha the same way the goblin was.

## Why the Party Stays a Few Days 🟡

Options. They can be combined.

- **The trail is held by goblins.** The road to the town runs past the goblins' territory, and the villagers won't risk it until a hunting party clears it, or until a trapper who knows a back way returns in a few days.
- **The send-off.** The leader announces a feast in three days to send off the next pilgrim pair, and begs the angels to bless them. Refusing would break the village's heart. This puts the PCs at the center of the leader's scheme.
- **Recovery.** The party is battered from the ambush and short on gear (Terek arrives under-equipped). The smith can make or mend gear, but it takes a few days.
- **A lead on their goals.** The leader claims to keep a holy relic or text from "the last angel." He'll show it at the feast. That hooks Lexandros and Reeve, the two looking for the Lost Book. Meldren might hear rumors of a "chain from heaven" somewhere in the hills.

**Recommendation:** combine the goblin-held trail (the hard reason) with the send-off feast (the hook).
