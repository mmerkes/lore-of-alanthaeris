## Where We Left Off

The party came through the Door into **ruins in a forest** on Iskiaeris. The session ended on the *thwap* of bowstrings.

## Outline

1. **Ambush.** Goblins attack in the ruins, more than the party can handle.
2. **Rescue.** Villagers arrive with meager weapons and fight the goblins alongside the party.
3. **Welcome.** The villagers greet the party warmly as angels, and take them home to the village.
4. **Something is off.** Over several days, the cult's strangeness surfaces bit by bit.
5. **First night:** the hag's clay dolls. See [The Hag](#the-hag).

## Scene 1: Goblin Ambush

✅ Open with combat against **more goblins than the party can handle**.

✅ **Surface goblins are nasty and want to kill them.** This is the first shock of Iskiaeris. In Alanthaeris, goblins are kind, sweet, and soft-spoken, like house elves. The PCs have never seen a goblin be cruel.

🟡 Ideas:
- The goblins use the ruins as a hunting ground, because things come through the Door.
- ✅ One goblin is **captured alive**. Deximus takes it back to the village to be burned at the stake.

## Scene 2: The Villagers Arrive

✅ Mid-fight, **villagers with meager weapons** (pitchforks, hatchets, slings) charge in and turn the battle.

✅ They are **cultists of a strange sect of Alantha**. They sincerely believe they're good people; their leader has manipulated them.

✅ They believe **anyone from the flying city is an angel sent down by Alantha**.

## The Village

✅ A **small, walled village in rough country**.
✅ A trail leads to a **bigger town, several days' journey** away.
✅ **No pub.** Instead, **each villager volunteers to take one PC home** to stay at their house. This splits the party at night, which matters for the hag.

❓ Village name. (Alanthan names are Greek; surface names can be something else.)
❓ Name of the bigger town.

## Village Characters

### Orshon, the Leader

✅ **Orshon** is an **evil priest** with a **sincere, dopey facade** that makes people root for him. He has **flashes of being strict, serious, and dangerous**.

✅ **His secret:** he feeds villagers to **a demon in the woods** near the village. Periodically he chooses a **pair**, such as a romantic couple, siblings, or best friends, to go on **pilgrimage** to spread the word of Alantha. They're never heard from again. Everyone assumes they're out seeing the world. They're dead.

✅ **He has information about Reeve**, and an excuse for why it will take time to gather. See [below](#what-orshon-knows-about-reeve).

🟡 At the table:
- He's delighted by everything. He calls people "friend" and laughs at his own jokes a beat too long.
- **His tell:** when someone questions the doctrine, the smile stays but his voice drops and goes flat. Then he's cheerful again, as if it never happened.
- The pilgrim pairs mirror Alanthaeris's own pilgrimage. Lexandros, a real pilgrim, will notice.

❓ The demon: what it is, what it gives him in return, and where its lair is.
❓ Does he believe any of it, or is the faith purely a tool?

### Deximus, the Right-Hand Man

✅ **Deximus** is Orshon's right hand. He's **skeptical of the PCs** and **negative**, which makes him inherently suspicious. He's also **very devout**, and **too enthusiastic about the burning**.

✅ **He's the one who takes the captured goblin back** for sacrifice.

🟡 Ideas:
- **He's sincere and doesn't know about the demon.** His suspicion is pointed at the wrong people. That makes him a red herring, and a possible late ally once the truth comes out, or a zealot who sides with Orshon anyway.
- **Why he doubts the angels:** they bleed, they argue, and they get the scripture "wrong."
- His threat, never quite said: *a true angel wouldn't burn.*
- He's the one who carves each new feather on the pilgrim post.

### What Orshon Knows About Reeve

✅ Orshon has information about Reeve, and an excuse for why it will take time to gather.

🟡 **Proposed: Reeve's uncle passed through.** Years ago another "angel" came through the Door and stayed in the village a while. (🟡 This is the uncle, Demetrios.) He left behind a **journal and pages of scripture**. That's where the village's garbled scripture comes from: decades of copying, reciting, and misremembering his pages. Orshon recognizes the Kallergis name, or Reeve's face, the moment he hears it.

🟡 **The excuse, options:**
1. **The pages are scattered.** The uncle's pages were treated as holy relics and **handed out among the families**. Each household keeps one or two, and each family must give theirs up willingly, in a rite, at the send-off feast. That takes days. It also means the host families may each have a page, which Reeve could find early by snooping.
2. **It's kept at a woodland shrine.** The journal is kept at a shrine out in the woods, and Orshon must fast and pray for three days before he can enter. 🔒 The shrine is near the demon's lair.
3. **Only one person knows.** The person who knew the uncle best is away, and won't be back for a few days.

**Recommendation:** option 1. It explains the garbled scripture, ties into the host families, and gives Reeve something to snoop for.

❓ What does Orshon actually know about where the uncle went, and is the uncle alive?

## What Makes the Cult Uncomfortable

✅ **They believe Alanthans are angels.**
✅ **They commune with Alantha through smoke.** They awkwardly burn things.
✅ **They have garbled scripture.** See [below](#garbled-scripture).

### Smoke Is How They Speak to Alantha

🟡 The surface sect took Alanthaeris's own doctrine literally: **lightness is holiness, and smoke is the lightest thing there is.** Burning something makes it light and sends it up to her. That makes the cult a twisted mirror of the faith rather than a random oddity, and Lexandros can recognize the logic.

- ✅ **The captured goblin is burned at the stake.** Deximus drags it back from the ambush. This is the first clear sign that something is off.
- 🟡 They burn written confessions, locks of hair, or treasured objects to shed weight, and read Alantha's answers in the shapes of the smoke.

### Garbled Scripture

✅ The villagers recite fragments of Alanthan scripture and sayings, but they're **wrong**: words are swapped, lines are out of order, and the meanings have drifted toward smoke and sacrifice. The PCs will recognize them, and that's what makes them unsettling.

🟡 **Suggestions.** Each one pairs the real version from [Alantha](../../../faith/alantha.md) with the village version.

| Alanthaeris says | The village says | What changed |
|---|---|---|
| "May the sky keep you." / "And you." | **"May the smoke take you."** / "And you." | The first greeting the PCs hear. It sounds warm and is said warmly. |
| "Go lightly." | **"Burn lightly."** | A blessing. |
| "Let what is heavy fall away." | **"Let what is heavy burn away."** | Said over the goblin at the stake. |
| "Pluck one feather and the wing fails." | **"Pluck one feather and the wing flies."** | 🔒 The heart of Orshon's doctrine: one sacrifice lifts the whole. It justifies the pilgrim pairs. |
| "Every feather in its place." | **"Every feather to the fire."** | Deximus's favorite. |
| "She took wing last winter." (died) | **"She took smoke last winter."** | They burn their dead. |
| *The Impossible Flame:* temple lamps burn downward. | The village hangs its lamps **upside down**, and they gutter and smoke. | They heard about a holy lamp and tried to copy it. |
| The Twelve are the feathers of Alantha's wing. | **Twelve pairs make a wing.** When twelve pilgrim pairs have gone up, Alantha will lift the village into the sky. | 🔒 Orshon's invention. A post in the village square has a feather carved for each pair sent, and **eleven** are carved. The coming send-off would be the twelfth. That's a ticking clock. |

🟡 **Table moment:** Lexandros quotes scripture correctly, and Deximus corrects him with the garbled version. Who's the real angel now?

🟡 **Where it came from:** the pages of an earlier pilgrim. See [Orshon's information on Reeve](#what-orshon-knows-about-reeve).

### Passed For Now

These were considered and set aside: collecting the angels' hair and clippings as relics, breath as holy, fasting to stay light (too grotesque for this table), ruinous generosity, and "angels go home by smoke."

## Why the Party Stays a Few Days 🟡

- ✅ **Orshon's information on Reeve** takes time to gather. See [above](#what-orshon-knows-about-reeve).
- 🟡 **The trail is held by goblins.** The road to town runs past the goblins' territory, and the villagers won't risk it until a hunting party clears it or a trapper who knows a back way returns.
- 🟡 **The send-off.** In three days there's a feast to send off the next pilgrim pair, the **twelfth**, and Orshon begs the angels to bless it. He'll hand over the uncle's pages there.
- 🟡 **Recovery.** The party is battered and short on gear (Terek arrived under-equipped). The smith needs a few days.
