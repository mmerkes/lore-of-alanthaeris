# The Twelve Houses

*A player's primer: what any Alanthan knows about the ruling families.*

Twelve great houses rule Alanthaeris. Each one descends from one of the founders who raised the city. The head of each house holds a seat among **the Twelve** and is called its **Keeper**. Six houses follow the **arcane** tradition and six follow the **divine**. Together they are the wings of the city: *pluck one feather and the wing fails.*

Every house has a traditional sphere of responsibility. Everyone knows which house to thank, or blame, for what.

## The Arcane Houses

| House | Keeper | Known for |
|---|---|---|
| **Evgenios** | Theophanes | Civic enchantments, magical infrastructure, the Door of Alantha |
| **Drakaina** | Melanippe | Wards, defensive magic, policing dangerous sorcery |
| **Asterion** | Praxion | Medicine, alchemy, scholarship |
| **Kallergis** | Theramenes | Levitation, weather-working, keeping the city aloft |
| **Myriantos** | Sophia | Divination, records, the census |
| **Pyrakles** | Leandros | Artifice, sacred metalwork, fire, industry |

## How the Houses Work

- **Seats are inherited,** but succession within a house isn't automatic. Heirs build their claims, often through marriage.
- **Marriage is politics.** Houses marry to bind arcane to divine and house to house. They also marry into useful families outside the Twelve, like the **Kyrenios** architects or the **Melanthios** artisans.
- **Names:** children usually take their father's surname. A Keeper house's name wins over a family with no seat, and a marriage contract can settle the question.
