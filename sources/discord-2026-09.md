# Discord Notes: September 2026

[← Home](../README.md)

These are the lore-relevant points from planning chats between Matt (GM) and Mike (player), summarized. Anything adopted as canon is recorded in the [Decision Log](../decisions.md).

## 9/11: The pilgrimage mission
- Matt: Give surface-goers a unifying "BS mission" to find something or someone prophesied, and not return until it's found. It should carry heavy symbolic significance for the faith. Evangelizing is secondary.
- Mike: Maybe it's a "second coming" prophecy.

## 9/12: A secret motive, and scale
- Mike: Maybe the pilgrims' ulterior motive is to find and kill the second coming, because he'd reunite the city with the surface.
- Matt: The pilgrims themselves wouldn't have that motive, but **the Twelve** might fear the prophet and want them dead. This could be one of the main reasons for the pilgrimage, along with getting rid of people they don't like. It's a closely kept secret that only the Twelve know.
- Scale reference: Avalir (Critical Role), with less mountain on top and more mountain below. The city is organized in three rings, like a hill town, with grander architecture toward the center.
- Architectural style is unclear; gather reference images.

## 9/13: Handoff
- Mike handed the doc to Matt: "not wedded to anything," so change or dump freely.
- Matt will formalize only what's pertinent (what the Twelve are, details of Mike's character's departure). The rest stays as source material.
- Mike wants to see the final version of what his character knows, but not the secrets.
- Matt has ideas about family dynamics, which may or may not come into play.

## 9/14: Naming and structure
- The city name moved away from "Alanthopolis." "Acropolis" ("high city") was considered. Other candidates Mike riffed: Aetheriopolis, Firmantis, Alticivitas, Volatilia, Caelathea, Ouranoupoli. **Alanthaeris** won, and it evokes Atlantis.
- A naming palette hasn't been set; Elvish was floated ("Taurost").
- Matt simplified some of the brainstorm's metaphors (dactyls, the loom).
- There's a possible NPC: a poetic priest who "flowers up" the canon, to the chagrin of the establishment.
- The rest of the 9/14 structure notes (High Priest, Keepers of the Book, Wardens, the Underspire) are in the [Decision Log](../decisions.md#2026-09-14).
