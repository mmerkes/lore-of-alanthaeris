# Mike's Brainstorm: The Alantha Religion

[← Home](../README.md)

> **Not canon.** This is Mike's original brainstorm, kept for inspiration and lightly formatted into Markdown. Mike handed it off on 9/13/26 and isn't attached to any of it. Anything adopted is recorded on the lore pages and in the [Decision Log](../decisions.md). Several ideas have since been superseded. For example, "Alanthopolis" became Alanthaeris, the Keepers of the Loom became the Keepers of the Book, and the Inquisitors, Wardens, and Acolytes were unified as Wardens.

## Origin Story: the Alantheogony
- **Prehistoric Era:** A primitive civilization originally worshipped a pantheon of gods, the size and names of which are no longer considered important by those in the faith.
- **The Outsider's Claim:** A radical, fanatic outsider (or messiah) arrived and claimed that the existing pantheon could not truly be gods. Instead, he asserted that the pantheon must derive their power from a singular wellspring or source, that this was in fact revealed to him through a series of divine reveries.
- **Monotheistic Divinity:** The messiah claimed divinity originates from a single monotheistic deity known as Alantha, the All-Mother. Only through the All-Mother can one truly be shepherded to the afterlife.
- **Growth of Following:** The messiah, whose name has been lost to the ages, was unpopular, reviled even, because people resisted the idea that their traditional gods were themselves somehow subordinate to or dependent upon another. However, the messiah gradually built a following over time nonetheless, particularly among those who felt betrayed or forsaken by the gods.
- **Gathering the Twelve:** A revelation was given to the messiah: gather 12 spellweavers, half divine and half arcane, to found a great temple worthy of Alantha, and a shining city in which Alantha's followers would want for nothing.
- **Ascension of the Flying City:** Upon executing the ritual, a massive plot was carved from the land and borne aloft high into the sky, with the messiah and all of Alantha's followers aboard.

## Alanthopolis: Social Structure & Temple
- **Name & Purpose:** Named Alanthopolis as a living monument and testament to the power of the All-Mother, Alantha. A great temple was built by her followers, around which grew settlements and eventually shops and farmland and so much more.

### Social Structure & Class Division
- **First Class / Innermost Dactyl (Ring):** Elite clerics, priests, and insiders who run the religion and govern the city. They live in pristine high towers, practicing sky-gazing, silent contemplation, and prayer to Alantha.
- **Second Class / Middle Dactyl:** Merchants, landowners, bankers, etc. They manage hydroponic farms, trade networks within Alanthopolis, and supply the clergy; they are held to ostentatious tithes to keep their businesses and shops solvent.
- **Third Class / Outer Dactyl:** Laborers.
- **Underclass / Underspire:** Worker bees, dissidents, prisoners, and other undesirables. They inhabit the dense "Underspire" beneath the city. They stay out of sight, fighting for resources, developing underground folklore, and hiding secret shrines to otherwise forgotten pantheon gods.

### Temple of Alantha Architecture
- **Spire of the Nexus:** The central grand cathedral built shaped like an inverted teardrop suspended directly over Alantha's floating core.
- **Acoustic Light Sanctuaries:** Temples feature open-air domes lined with resonance crystals that chime harmonic chords when mountain winds pass through them.
- **Glass-Floor Sanctums:** Prayer chambers built with crystalline floors offering terrifying, vertigo-inducing views of the abyss below, reminding worshippers of their elevation.

## Deities & Celestial Entities
- **The All-Mother (Alantha):** The new monotheistic god created through the ritual.
- **The Original Pantheon:** Ancient gods worshipped by primitive civilization whose power was siphoned away to construct/birth Alantha.

## Hierarchy & Organizational Structure
- **The Messiah:** The radical outsider prophet of Alantha. Long dead. Name & origin unknown. Dissidents believe he was a demon or devil who walked the earth and tricked true believers in deifying him as the false god, Alantha.
- **The Eminent, or The Apex (High Priest/Priestess):** The supreme spiritual leader who is said to be the sole mortal that can communicate directly with Alantha's divine presence.
- **The Twelve, or the Keepers of the Loom (Domain: Arcane-Divine Synthesis):** High-ranking priests responsible for maintaining magical harmony and studying the Twelvefold Codex. There must *always* be 12 divine and arcane spellweavers in this body. This is not only a symbolic nod to the city's founding ritual; the Twelve also constitute the highest council of the faith. They are selected from the most promising, aspiring, devoted clergy, known as The Grosse of Underkeepers, or The Hundred-Forty-Four. The long-dead original Twelve are known as the Blessed Saints.
- **Inquisitors of the Anchor (Domain: Order & Stability):** Ecclesiastical judges who maintain social class order and root out surface-heresy. They are the enforcers of the faith. Their job is to keep order, to preserve the official dogma, and to handle / squash dissidents. They have their own security force called the Anchor Bearers.
- **Wardens of the Threshold (Domain: The Door):** Clerics stationed permanently at the Door of Alantha, charged with ritual preparation of Chosen Envoys.
- **Acolytes of the Cloud (Domain: Sustenance & Purity):** Lower-ranking clergy in charge of water purification, air blessings (e.g. the Rite of the First Breath) and urban rites.
- **The Chosen Envoys:** Select individuals chosen during holy observances to journey through the Door of Alantha to proselytize on the surface, facing unexpected danger upon arrival. Also known as All-Mother's Pilgrims.

## Rituals, Rites, & Ceremonies
- **The Sacred Synthesis Ritual:** A ritual revealed to the messiah directly by Alantha.
  - *Participants:* Requires 12 spellweavers representing both divine and arcane domains, gathered by the messiah.
  - *True Purpose & Effect:* Regardless of whether the messiah and the spellweavers are aware of it, the ritual actually siphons power directly from all the existing gods in the pantheon to deify the All-Mother, Alantha—the very deity they already believed in.
  - *Execution Outcome:* Creation of the giant sky-bound city of Alanthopolis. These original twelve weavers are now known as the Blessed Saints.
- **The Rite of Reconstitution:** Unbeknownst to none but the innermost circle of the faith, the Twelve are crucial to the ongoing well-being of Alanthopolis. Every so often (every year? every century? etc.), the Twelve must gather to re-synthesize their arcane and divine magics to keep Alantha potent by siphoning might from the other gods.
- **The Divine Descent Ceremony:**
  - *Frequency:* Occurs on a regular, recurring cycle (e.g., periodic interval, every year or five or ten or hundred or whatever).
  - *The Pilgrimage/Mission:* A chosen few individuals are selected during this ceremony to pass through the Door of Alantha, a one-way portal.
  - *Objective & Disillusionment:* The chosen envoys enter the surface believing they will be welcomed warmly to spread the word of Alantha, only to face a hostile, dangerous hellscape unprepared.
- **The Rite of the First Breath (Infant Dedication):** Newborns are brought to the highest available balcony in their district to breathe the open sky air and receive a blessing from an Acolyte of the Cloud.
- **The Trial of the Abyss (Coming-of-Age Rite):** At age 16, youths must spend a night standing vigil over a glass-floor sanctum looking down at the void; facing the fear of falling signifies faith in Alantha's levitation.
- **The Ordainment of the Cloth (Clergy Initiation):** Initiate priests undergo a 12-day fast in total silence before taking an oath where twelve threads of different hues are woven into their ceremonial robes, tying them permanently to the priesthood of Alantha.
- **The Anointing at the Gate (Envoy Consecration):** All-Mother's Pilgrims, the chosen envoys selected for the Door of Alantha undergo anointing with sacred cloud-oils (received through Alantha's magics) and receive a blessing from a Warden of the Threshold before stepping through the portal.

## Funerary & Burial Traditions
- **The Sky-Return Rite:** The elite First Class (Inner Dactyl) are ritually cremated, and their ashes are dispersed into the upper winds where they are believed to remain perpetually above Alanthopolis.
- **Encapsulated Memory Stones:** The Second Class (Middle Dactyl) have their remains condensed into small glowing crystals woven into family memorial chandeliers.
- **The Dropped Burial (Heretical/Pauper Rite):** Laborers who die without paying burial tithes are quietly dropped into the Underspire—dogmatically framed as "returning them to the dust."

## Core Beliefs & Tenets
- **Evangelism to the Surface:** Spreading the faith of Alantha to the surface world is a sacred duty entrusted to selected envoys during periodic holy ceremonies, based on the dogma that surface dwellers await salvation. Alanthans believe that the only road to the afterlife leads through Alantha. Non-believers are effectively queuing up in some liminal space, and the pilgrims who descend do so to welcome people into the faith to shepherd them through (even retroactively if the family accepts Alantha's blessing).
- **Myth vs. Surface Reality:** The religious doctrine promises a welcoming reception on the surface, masking the grim reality of the surface's devolution into a hellscape.

## Symbology & Iconography
- **The Twelve-Spoked Loom:** The central holy symbol of the faith—a circular emblem with twelve radiating spokes converging upon a single glowing central gem, representing the Blessed Saints, the twelve spellweavers channeling their power into Alantha.
- **The Threshold Arch:** A stylized archway symbol worn as amulets by pilgrims and clerics, symbolizing the Door of Alantha and passage between realms.
- **The Impossible Flame:** Iconography depicting flames pointing downward toward a floating city, symbolizing the divine lift and separation from the terrestrial world.

## Sacred Texts & Scriptures
- **The Book of Synthesis:** The foundational scripture detailing the ascension and the merging of the old pantheon's power into Alantha.
- **The Liturgy of the Gate:** Ritual guidelines and prayers chanted by the Wardens of the Threshold during the anointment of the pilgrims and the opening of the Door of Alantha.
- **The Twelvefold Codex:** A sacred compilation of canonical chants, domain formulas, and philosophical meditations recorded by the original Twelve spellweavers, the Blessed Saints. This is shared only among the innermost circle within the faith: the Apex and the Keepers of the Loom.
- **The Breath of the Firmament:** Daily devotional breviary containing morning, noon, and evening prayers used across all social classes.

## Epithets & Nicknames for Alantha
- **The All-Mother:** Her primary, universally recognized holy title.
- **The Sky-Loom:** Referring to her role in weaving together divine and arcane threads of power.
- **The Wellspring:** Emphasizing her position as the true source of divinity.
- **The Bitch-Queen:** A secret, derogatory epithet used among heretical surface survivors and Underspire dissidents (e.g., the Dustbound).

## Everyday Speech, Idioms, & Prayer Touchstones
- **"By the Firmament's Grace":** A common exclamation of relief or gratitude.
- **"May the Loom Keep You":** A standard blessing exchanged upon parting or traveling.
- **"Heavier than Surface-Dust":** An idiom used to describe something utterly worthless or burdensome.
- **"As the Door (or Gate) Wills":** A phrase expressing resignation to fate or divine providence.
- **"May She Hold Us High":** A short prayer touchstone uttered during turbulence, structural tremors, or anxiety.

## Noteworthy Historical Events
- **The First Betrayal (Year 42 Ascension Era (A.E.)):** A movement of heretics and dissidents built unbeknownst to the clergy of Alantha, led by a mad priest who had been denied entry into the Underkeepers. The rebellion was brutally put down and resulted in creation of the Inquisitors of the Anchor and the physical separation of laborers into the Lower Dactyl and the banishment of all rebels and their family members to the Underspire.
- **The Guild-Clergy Accord (Year 115 A.E.):** A major merchant uprising resulted in a formal treaty granting trade guilds exclusive rights to landownership and city commerce in exchange for heavy religious tithes, establishing the Second Class (Middle Dactyl).
- **The Schism of the Seventh Spellweaver (Year 210 A.E.):** One of the original Twelve's lineage attempted to breach the Door of Alantha early; the event was suppressed, leading to the creation of the Wardens of the Threshold and rigid enforcement of the three-tier caste system (plus the undercaste who exist out of sight but not out of mind).

## Theological Heresies & Factions
- **The Dustbound:** Secret third-class & undercaste dissidents who believe the surface is not a hellscape to be converted, but an original paradise forsaken by the Messiah.

## Modern Era & Connection to Surface
- **Isolation from the Surface:** In the modern era, there is no physical or regular connection between the citizens living in the floating city of Alanthopolis and the surface world below that the land originally broke away from.
- **The Door (or Gate) of Alantha:** The sole means of travel between the sky city and the planet's surface is a magical gateway known as the Door of Alantha, a portal connecting the city above to the world below. No one, and nothing, has ever come back through the door.
- **Reality of the Surface World:**
  - *The Modern Surface Hellscape:* Contrary to the myths and historical narratives taught in the Alantha faith, the surface world has changed radically since prehistoric times and is now a brutal, desolate hellscape.
  - *Hostility to Evangelism:* While the chosen envoys are taught that surface dwellers will welcome them and be eager to embrace Alantha, the reality is starkly opposite: surface inhabitants are hostile, hardened, and completely unreceptive to hearing about Alantha or converting to the religion.

## Open Questions (from the brainstorm)
- If envoys pass through the Door of Alantha and never return, how does the priesthood maintain the narrative that envoys are successfully welcomed? Maybe no one questions it?
- What keeps the surface dwellers, and their gods, from doing a counter-ritual to weaken Alantha and reclaim their power? Perhaps the dark clouds that separate Alanthopolis from the surface exude a sort of anti-magic that prevents such powerful rituals from happening? (Perhaps Alantha herself was responsible for creating the anti-magic barrier when the city first lifted off.)
