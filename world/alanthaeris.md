# Alanthaeris

[← Home](../README.md)

✅ **Name:** *Alanthaeris*, which joins the goddess's name with a sky root. It echoes "Atlantis."

## Shape & Scale

✅ Alanthaeris is a huge island of earth and stone that was torn from the land and carried into the sky. Most of the island's rock hangs **below** the city, like an inverted mountain. The city on top is laid out like a **hill town** in **three concentric rings**, and the architecture gets grander toward the center.

- Scale reference: Avalir from Critical Role, but with less mountain on top and more underneath.
- ❓ Architectural style is still undecided. Collect reference images.

## The Rings

| Ring | Who lives there | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Inner Ring** | The Twelve, the High Priest, the High Temple of Alantha | ✅ grandest; ❓ name |
| **Middle Ring** | ❓ Merchants, guilds, landowners | ❓ |
| **Outer Ring** | ❓ Laborers | ❓ |

❓ The ring names are open. Mike's brainstorm called them "Dactyls," but that idea was simplified. See [Open Questions](../open-questions.md#the-city).

## The Underspire

✅ **The Underspire** is the name for everything **beneath** the city, inside the hanging rock.

- ✅ **The Great Works:** the central core, home to the **dwarves**, and where the city's machinery is. It's led by the **Master of the Great Works**.
- ✅ **The Rookery:** the outer area, home to the poor, **goblins**, and criminals. It's named after Victorian slums, and birds nest there too.

## Getting Down

✅ **The Door** is the way to the surface that pilgrims use. ❓ Whether it's the *only* way, and whether it's one-way, are both open.
