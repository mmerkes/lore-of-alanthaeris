# Iskiaeris

[← Home](../README.md)

✅ **Iskiaeris** is the surface world below Alanthaeris. Everyone who descends arrives through **the Door**.

## Landing

✅ The Door lets out into **ruins in a forest**.

## Peoples

✅ **Goblins** on the surface are vicious and want to kill outsiders. They're nothing like the gentle goblins of the Rookery.

✅ **Swiftwell**, the village nearest the Door, is small and walled, in rough country. A trail leads to **Redmere**, a bigger town several days away.

## Redmere

✅ **Redmere** is the bigger town, several days down the trail from Swiftwell.

✅ **It isn't very pious.** Faith there is casual at best, a sharp contrast with Swiftwell.

🟡 The rest of this section is proposed.

- **A market town** at a river crossing, where the river runs red with iron-rich clay. It's the nearest real town to Swiftwell, several days down the trail.
- **Trade:** hill folk bring furs, timber, and honey down, and go home with salt, iron, and cloth.
- **It has an inn,** unlike Swiftwell. 🟡 Name it when the party gets there.
- **What the town thinks of Swiftwell:** odd, pious hill folk who keep to themselves. Townsfolk call them "smoke-eaters." Harmless, as far as anyone knows.
- **Faith:** Redmere doesn't follow Swiftwell's sect. 🟡 Any gods it does keep are an afterthought.
- **Authority:** ❓ a lord's steward, a council of guild masters, or a small garrison?
